Research prototype — decision support only. ASTRA estimates cognitive load from physiological signals. It is not a medical or psychological diagnostic system and does not detect cardiovascular disease or orthostatic intolerance. Model trained on the NASA Flight Crew Physiological Data for Crew State Monitoring dataset (6 pilots); the recordings are simulator and benchmark sessions, not spaceflight.
